# Walmart Inc. (WMT) — Company Research Report

## Company Snapshot
Walmart Inc. is an American multinational omnichannel retail corporation operating hypermarkets (Supercenters), discount department stores, grocery stores (Neighborhood Markets), pharmacies, gas stations, and warehouse clubs (Sam's Club). It is headquartered in Bentonville, Arkansas, incorporated in Delaware, and trades on Nasdaq under the ticker **WMT** (SIC 5331, Retail-Variety Stores). The business is organized into four divisions: Walmart U.S., Walmart International, Sam's Club, and Global eCommerce. Walmart operates in the United States and 19 other countries.

## Context from Wikipedia (history and markets)
- **Founding and early history:** Sam Walton opened the first Wal-Mart Discount City in Rogers, Arkansas on July 2, 1962, building on his earlier Walton's Five and Dime. The company incorporated in 1969 and went public on the NYSE in 1972.
- **National and international expansion:** Walmart became the largest U.S. retailer by revenue in 1990 and expanded internationally into Mexico (1991), Canada (1994), South America (1995), the UK via Asda (1999), and later into China, Japan, India (via Flipkart, 2018), and Africa (via Massmart, 2010). It has exited markets like Germany (2006), South Korea (2006), and sold majority stakes in Asda, Seiyu, and Walmart Brazil.
- **Markets today:** Walmart operates globally with ~10,373 stores and 601 warehouse clubs. Divisions include Walmart U.S. (Supercenters, Discount Stores, Neighborhood Markets), Walmart International (19 countries), Sam's Club, and Global eCommerce (based in San Bruno, CA). Key competitors include Amazon, Costco, Target, Kroger, and Aldi.
- **Governance and recent events:** Greg Penner is chairman; John Furner became president and CEO (succeeding Doug McMillon, who announced his step-down in November 2025). Walmart shifted its listing from NYSE to Nasdaq in December 2025 and was added to the Nasdaq-100 in January 2026. In early 2026 Walmart became the first traditional retailer with a $1 trillion market cap.
